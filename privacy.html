Your privacy.

How this website preview handles information and connects you with Zenith Hospitality Global.

Preview notice

This notice describes the current local prototype. Zenith will confirm the final policy, service providers, retention periods and privacy contact arrangements before launch.

Updated 7 November 2026

Project enquiries

The enquiry form uses the name, email address, project details and message you enter to prepare an email draft in your browser. The website does not send the draft or submit it to an enquiry database. You choose whether to copy the draft or open your email app and send it.

Email and external services

If you send an email, your email provider and Zenith’s email service handle that message. WhatsApp, LinkedIn, the existing Zenith blog and the external recruitment portal operate under their own privacy arrangements when you follow their links. Job applications are handled separately by the recruitment provider.

Website resources

This preview loads fonts from Google Fonts and the Tailwind browser script from jsDelivr. Those providers receive the connection information needed to deliver their resources, such as an IP address and browser request details. Other imagery and animation libraries are served locally by this prototype.

Measurement and browser storage

No analytics or advertising integration has been installed in this prototype. The enquiry code does not save form entries to local storage or cookies. Browser features such as autofill and history are controlled by your browser.

Questions about information you share

Contact hello@zenith-hospitality.com with privacy questions or to discuss information you have sent to Zenith. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in a general project enquiry.
